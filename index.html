Built on Microsoft Technologies

SKYTS® is engineered using C#, .NET, Blazor and Microsoft SQL Server as its core technology stack. Together, these technologies support a modular foundation for building, running and evolving business applications.

The SKYTS® Framework extends this foundation with reusable UI components, custom data access, application runtime services and development tools.

  • C#
  • .NET

Extended with MudBlazor

MudBlazor supports the user interface layer, while SKYTS® builds upon it with its own reusable UI components, consistent application behavior, custom data access, runtime services and development tools.

Framework Applications

One Connected Framework.

SKYTS® brings application development, business operations and system lifecycle management together through three specialized applications. Each serves a distinct purpose—building business solutions, running them efficiently and managing their deployment, maintenance and continuous evolution.

  • BUILD

    SkytsStudio

    Application authoring, metadata definitions, project configuration, code generation and release preparation.

  • RUN

    SkytsRuntime

    Application runtime environment, authentication, navigation, sessions and integrated application functionality.

  • MANAGE

    SkytsAdmin

    Application installation, deployment, upgrades, synchronization, verification and lifecycle management.

Application Solutions

Business Applications Built on SKYTS®

One Technology Foundation. Solutions for Different Industries.

SKYTS® brings together reusable technology and practical industry knowledge to develop business applications for educational institutions, manufacturing enterprises and workforce management. Each solution addresses its own business requirements while benefiting from a common technology foundation.

SKYTS® EIMS

Educational Institute Management System

Simplifying academic administration and institutional operations through connected student, academic and fee management processes.

Solution Capabilities

  • Admissions Management — Student enquiries, registration and admission.
  • Academic Management — Courses, classes, batches and academic sessions.
  • Student Information — Student profiles, institutional records and academic information.
  • Fee Management — Fee structures, collections, receipts and outstanding dues.
  • Financial Operations — Student fee accounting and related financial reporting.
  • Institutional Reporting — Administrative reports and operational information.

SKYTS® ERP

Enterprise Resource Planning

Connecting business processes across customer orders, procurement, inventory, manufacturing and warehouse operations.

Solution Capabilities

  • Order Management — Customer orders, work orders and order tracking.
  • Material Planning — Material requirements planning and procurement requirements.
  • Purchase Management — Requisitions, purchase orders and supplier processes.
  • Inventory Management — Stock control, stores and material movement.
  • Manufacturing Management — Production planning, routing and work-in-progress.
  • Warehouse & Dispatch — Warehouse operations, packing and dispatch.
  • Business Reporting — Operational monitoring and management information.

PAY-BUILDER®

Human Resources & Payroll Management

Supporting employee administration, workforce attendance and payroll operations through connected HR processes.

Solution Capabilities

  • Employee Management — Employee records, departments and designations.
  • Employee Lifecycle — Onboarding and offboarding.
  • Workforce Scheduling — Shifts, duty rosters and work locations.
  • Attendance Management — Daily and monthly attendance, including attendance-data integration.
  • Leave & Overtime — Leave administration and overtime records.
  • Payroll Management — Salary definitions, payroll calculations and processing.
  • HR Reporting — Employee, attendance and payroll information.
